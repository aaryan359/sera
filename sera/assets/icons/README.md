# Settings icon assets

Provider logos are downloaded from Google's official `gstatic.com` product image
assets. They identify their respective integrations; they do not imply partnership
or a connected account. Product names and logos belong to Google LLC.

Utility SVG images are from Lucide's official repository, under the license in
`settings/LICENSE`. Their stroke color is adjusted to match Sera's interface.
No icon font is required by the Settings screen or its panels.

Exact download URLs are recorded in `sources.json`.
